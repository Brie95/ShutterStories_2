date: 2026-10-01
---

Lenses shape the look and feel of every photograph. They determine how much of a scene you capture, how close you appear to the subject, and how much background blur you can create. Understanding lens types helps photographers choose the right tool for portraits, travel, and creative work.

## Prime Lenses
Prime lenses have a fixed focal length, such as 35mm or 50mm. They are known for sharp images and wide apertures, making them ideal for portraits and low‑light photography. See examples of portrait work in [[portrait-photography]].

## Zoom Lenses
Zoom lenses offer flexibility by covering multiple focal lengths. A 24–70mm lens is perfect for travel and everyday shooting, while a 70–200mm lens is great for sports and wildlife.

## Wide-Angle Lenses
Wide‑angle lenses capture more of the scene, making them excellent for landscapes and city photography. Explore how wide angles enhance travel images in [[travel-photography]].

## Telephoto Lenses
Telephoto lenses magnify distant subjects and compress backgrounds, creating dramatic portrait and landscape effects.

---

Choosing the right lens is one of the most important decisions a photographer makes. Each lens offers a unique perspective and creative opportunity.
