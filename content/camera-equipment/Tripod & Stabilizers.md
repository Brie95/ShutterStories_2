date: 2026-10-01

Tripods and stabilizers help photographers achieve sharp, steady images. Whether shooting long exposures, night scenes, or video, stability is essential for professional results.

## Tripods
Tripods keep your camera still during long exposures, landscape photography, and low‑light situations. They are especially useful when capturing city scenes at night, as shown in [[travel-photography]].

## Monopods
Monopods offer partial stability while remaining lightweight and portable. They are great for sports, wildlife, and events where mobility matters.

## Handheld Stabilizers
Gimbals and handheld stabilizers help create smooth video footage. They reduce camera shake when walking or moving, making them ideal for travel vlogs and cinematic shots.

## When to Use Stabilization
Use stabilization when:
- Shooting at slow shutter speeds  
- Recording video  
- Capturing long exposures  
- Photographing in low light  

---

Stability tools help photographers produce crisp, professional images across all shooting environments.
