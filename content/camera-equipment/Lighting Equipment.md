title: Lighting Equipment
date: 2026-10-01
---

Lighting equipment allows photographers to shape and control light. Whether shooting portraits, products, or outdoor scenes, understanding lighting tools is essential for creating mood and clarity.

## Reflectors
Reflectors bounce light back onto the subject, reducing shadows and brightening facial features. They are commonly used in portrait sessions like those in [[portrait-photography]].

## Diffusers
Diffusers soften harsh light, creating gentle, flattering illumination. They are perfect for outdoor portraits and product photography.

## LED Panels
LED panels provide continuous light that is easy to adjust. They are useful for both photography and video work.

## External Flashes
External flashes offer powerful bursts of light that help freeze motion and brighten dark environments. They are essential for indoor portrait sessions.

---

Lighting tools give photographers control over mood, contrast, and clarity, making them essential for professional‑quality images.
