Reliable storage ensures that your photos are saved quickly and safely. Memory cards and backup systems are essential parts of every photographer’s workflow.

## Memory Cards
Choose memory cards with fast write speeds, especially when shooting in RAW or using burst mode. Slow cards can cause delays or missed shots.

## External Hard Drives
External drives provide secure backup for large photo libraries. They are ideal for organizing travel and portrait sessions from [[travel-photography]] and [[portrait-photography]].

## Cloud Storage
Cloud storage offers remote access and protection against hardware failure. Many photographers use cloud backups to store edited images from [[photo-editing]].

## Best Practices
- Always format cards in your camera  
- Back up photos in multiple locations  
- Replace old or unreliable cards  
- Keep storage organized by project or category  

---

Good storage habits protect your work and ensure a smooth editing workflow.
