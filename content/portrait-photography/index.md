## Related Categories

Portrait photography is all about capturing personality, emotion, and presence. Whether the setting is outdoors, in a studio, or in a dramatic landscape, the goal is always the same: to tell a story through the subject. These examples highlight how environment, lighting, and composition work together to create compelling portraits. Portrait photography often relies on thoughtful editing techniques, which you can learn more about in the [[photo-editing/index|Photo Editing]] category.
