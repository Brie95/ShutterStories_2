Depth of field refers to how much of an image appears sharp. It is controlled by aperture, distance, and lens choice. Understanding depth of field helps photographers create focus and storytelling.

## Shallow Depth of Field
A wide aperture (like f/1.8) creates a blurry background and sharp subject. This is ideal for portraits and close‑ups. See examples in [[portrait-photography]].

## Deep Depth of Field
A narrow aperture (like f/11) keeps the entire scene sharp. This is perfect for landscapes and travel photography, as shown in [[travel-photography]].

## How to Control Depth of Field
- Use wide apertures for blur  
- Use narrow apertures for clarity  
- Step closer to your subject  
- Choose lenses with longer focal lengths  

---

Depth of field helps photographers guide attention and create emotional impact.
