Lighting shapes the mood, clarity, and emotion of a photograph. Understanding how light behaves helps photographers create intentional, expressive images.

## Natural Light
Natural light is soft, flattering, and easy to work with. Golden hour provides warm tones, while cloudy days offer diffused, even lighting. See natural light examples in [[portrait-photography]].

## Artificial Light
Artificial light includes lamps, LED panels, and flashes. These tools help control brightness, direction, and color temperature. Learn more in [[camera-equipment]].

## Direction of Light
Light direction affects shadows and depth:
- **Front light** reduces shadows  
- **Side light** adds drama  
- **Backlight** creates silhouettes  

## Color Temperature
Warm light feels cozy and emotional, while cool light feels calm and modern. Adjusting white balance helps maintain accurate colors.

---

Mastering lighting techniques allows photographers to create mood and highlight their subjects with intention.
