Composition is the foundation of strong photography. It determines how elements are arranged within the frame and how the viewer’s eye moves through the image. Good composition helps create balance, focus, and visual interest.

## Rule of Thirds
The rule of thirds divides the frame into nine equal sections. Placing your subject along these lines creates natural balance and avoids static, centered compositions. See how this applies in [[portrait-photography]].

## Leading Lines
Leading lines guide the viewer’s eye toward the subject. Roads, fences, rivers, and architectural lines are common examples. Explore leading lines in real scenes in [[travel-photography]].

## Framing
Framing uses natural elements—like windows, trees, or doorways—to draw attention to the subject. It adds depth and context to the image.

---

Strong composition transforms simple scenes into compelling photographs. Mastering these basics will improve every style of photography.
