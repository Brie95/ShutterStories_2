---
title: Perspective and Angles
date: 2026-10-01
---

Perspective changes how a viewer experiences a scene. By adjusting angles and distance, photographers can create dramatic, unique, or intimate images.

## High Angle
Shooting from above makes subjects appear smaller or more vulnerable. It’s useful for storytelling and environmental portraits.

## Low Angle
Low angles create power and drama. They make buildings, people, and landscapes appear larger and more impressive. See examples in [[travel-photography]].

## Eye Level
Eye‑level shots feel natural and balanced. They are commonly used in portraits and everyday photography.

## Creative Angles
Experimenting with diagonal lines, reflections, or unusual viewpoints adds creativity and interest to your images.

---

Perspective is one of the easiest ways to transform an ordinary photo into something memorable.
