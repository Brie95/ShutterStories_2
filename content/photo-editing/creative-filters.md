# Creative Filters

Filters add style, emotion, and personality to your photos. They can transform a simple image into something cinematic or nostalgic.

## Types of Filters

Filters range from subtle color shifts to dramatic transformations. They’re often used in mobile apps like VSCO, Lightroom Mobile, or Snapseed.

### When to Use Filters

Filters work best when they enhance your story rather than overpower it.

- Vintage filters
    
- Cinematic tones
    
- Black & white
    
- High contrast
