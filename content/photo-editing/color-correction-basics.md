# Color Correction Basics

Color correction ensures your photo looks natural and balanced. It’s one of the most important steps in photo editing because color affects mood, realism, and viewer perception.

## Understanding White Balance

White balance adjusts the temperature and tint of your image. A warm photo feels cozy, while a cool photo feels calm or dramatic.

### Tools for Color Correction

Most editing apps include sliders for temperature, tint, vibrance, and saturation.
