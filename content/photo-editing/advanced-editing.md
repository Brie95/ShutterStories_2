# Advanced Editing Tools

Once you master the basics, advanced tools help you refine your images with precision. These tools give you full control over lighting, color, and detail.

## Curves & Levels

Curves allow you to adjust brightness and contrast across specific tonal ranges. Levels help you control shadows, midtones, and highlights.

### Local Adjustments

Local edits target specific areas of your photo using brushes, masks, or gradients.
