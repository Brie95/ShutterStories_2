# Retouching Techniques

Retouching is the art of refining small details to enhance your subject. It’s commonly used in portrait photography but applies to any image where distractions need removal.

## Skin Retouching

Use tools like the healing brush or clone stamp to remove blemishes. Avoid over‑smoothing — natural texture is essential.

### Object Removal

Distracting elements like trash cans, stray hairs, or background clutter can be removed with content‑aware tools.
