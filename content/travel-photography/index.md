## SanDiego
![[../assets/SanDiego.JPG]]
San Diego offers a softer, coastal feel. This photo highlights how natural light, open skies, and relaxed architecture create a calm visual story — perfect for practicing composition and horizon balance.
## DowntownHouston
![[../assets/DowntownHouston.JPG]]
Houston’s downtown skyline shows the contrast between modern architecture and dramatic lighting. This image is a strong example of using leading lines and structure to guide the viewer’s eye through an urban landscape.
